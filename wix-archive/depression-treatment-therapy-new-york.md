## Understanding the Common Causes of Depression 

Depression has various causes, with genetics playing a significant role—those with a family history of depression are up to three times more likely to develop it. Women are particularly more susceptible to depression than men, and environmental factors can also contribute significantly. Experiencing abusive conditions, witnessing crime or drug use, neglect, or poverty can increase the risk of depression. Research also shows that children exposed to violence or extreme poverty may face structural changes in their brains, impacting their ability to manage anxiety and emotional stability as they grow.
