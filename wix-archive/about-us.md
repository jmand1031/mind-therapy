# Meet the Mind Therapy Team 

Our team of experts specialize in anxiety, depression, trauma, eating disorders, and more. Explore out staff below and find the therapist that is right for you. 

# ALLY NEKTALOV, 
LCSW, MASTER CASAC 

Director of Mind Therapy 

Ally is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker/Psychotherapist with an MSW (Master’s of Social Work) from New York University and holds a certificate from New York State as an Master CASAC (Certified Alcohol and Substance Abuse Counselor). She also completed Psychoanalytic Psychotherapy training at the Alanson J. White Institute; which uses an interpersonal psychoanalytic model. 

Ally is an Adjunct Professor at New York University and Touro College teaching Clinical Practice for social work graduate students. 

Prior to Mind Therapy Ally was the Director and Clinical Supervisor of an outpatient clinic treating substance use operating under a Federal Grant. Clients in this program participated in individual and/or group psychotherapy addressing triggers to substance use and past trauma. She was responsible for program operations, administering the grant, and supervised staff while ensuring quality care and compliance. 

In her free time, Ally enjoys practicing yoga, boxing, and playing tennis. She likes to cook and explore vegan restaurants with friends and family. Ally is a dog mom to baby Capri, a toy poodle. 

## Nixon Jara, MHC-P 

Mental Health Counselor Permit 

Nixon is a bilingual clinician who provides therapy in both English and Spanish and is passionate about helping adolescents, young adults, couples, and families navigate life’s challenges with greater confidence, connection, and self-understanding. 
​ 
Throughout his clinical training, Nixon has worked with clients experiencing anxiety, depression, trauma, relationship difficulties, family conflict, self-esteem concerns, and major life transitions. His experience includes individual therapy, couples counseling, intake assessments, treatment planning, and crisis and safety planning. At the heart of his work is a commitment to meeting clients where they are and creating a space where they feel heard, understood, and supported. 
Coming from a family of immigrants, Nixon understands that culture, family experiences, identity, and personal values can deeply influence the way we see ourselves and relate to others. He takes a culturally responsive and collaborative approach to therapy, seeking to understand each client’s individual perspective rather than making assumptions about their experiences. 
Nixon has a particular interest in working with adolescents and young adults as they navigate identity development, academic and career pressures, relationships, family dynamics, and the transition into adulthood. He strives to help clients develop greater self-awareness, strengthen coping skills, and feel more confident making decisions that align with who they are and who they want to become. 
​ 
He is also passionate about working with couples who want to improve their relationship and feel more connected. Nixon helps partners identify unhealthy patterns, strengthen communication, increase emotional understanding, navigate conflict more effectively, and work toward building healthier, more secure relationships. 
Nixon’s therapeutic style is empathetic, approachable, and collaborative. Using culturally responsive and evidence-based approaches, his goal is to empower clients to better understand themselves and their relationships while developing the tools needed to create meaningful and lasting change. 
​ 

## Kameeka Burke, MHC-P 

Mental Health Counselor Permit 

Kameeka is a mental health counseling professional with over 7 years of experience supporting children, adolescents, families, and school communities throughout New York City. With a strong background in both clinical counseling and educational settings, she specializes in helping young people navigate emotional, behavioral, academic, and social challenges through compassionate, developmentally informed care.

Kameeka has extensive experience working within NYC Public Schools, providing prevention and intervention services, crisis support, social-emotional learning, family engagement, and behavioral support for students from Pre-K through middle school. Her therapeutic approach integrates evidence-based modalities including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), mindfulness-based interventions, Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT), motivational interviewing, and trauma-informed care. She is particularly passionate about supporting underserved youth, early childhood mental health, emotional regulation, anxiety, self-esteem, behavioral concerns, family transitions, and school-related stressors. 
Kameeka works best with: 

• Children and adolescents (ages 4–18) 
• Parents and caregivers seeking support for behavioral or emotional concerns 
• Families navigating school, developmental, or transition-related challenges 
• Young adults experiencing anxiety, stress, emotional overwhelm, or relationship difficulties 
Known for her warm, engaging, and collaborative style, Kameeka creates a safe and supportive space where clients feel seen, empowered, and equipped with practical tools for growth and healing.

## Tamlyn Freedman, LMSW 

Licensed Master Social Worker 

Tamlyn is a licensed psychotherapist holding a Master’s of Social Work degree from New York University. In her work as a therapist, Tamlyn compassionately supports clients wishing to address a variety of concerns, including, anxiety, depression, life transitions, relationships, career changes, family issues, parenting, self-esteem, and grief. She strives to create a space where clients can feel safe to express themselves and share openly. 
​ 
Her therapeutic experience includes working with individuals, couples, and families of all ages and backgrounds. Prior to becoming a psychotherapist, Tamlyn received her Master of Science in Pharmaceutical Sciences and worked in clinical research. 
​ 
In addition to providing a gentle, warm, and empathetic ear, where appropriate, Tamlyn draws on evidence-based techniques rooted in a variety of modalities, including, cognitive behavioral therapy, acceptance and commitment therapy, solution-focused therapy, and emotion-focused therapy to best support the needs of her clients. She takes a client-centered and strengths-based approach to therapy, working collaboratively with each client to identify their unique strengths and needs to help direct the course of treatment. 
​ 
In her free time, Tamlyn enjoys running, baking bread, going to the theater, and spending time with her family and friends. 

## BARBARA HOFFMANN, LCSW-R, PHD, CASAC 

Licensed Clinical Social Worker 

Barbara is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker/Psychotherapist; dually licensed in New York and Florida; with an MSW (Masters of Social Work) from Hunter College School of Social Work; and holds a credential from New York State as a Master CASAC (Certified Alcohol and Substance Abuse Counselor). She also has a Ph. D. from John Jay (CUNY) in Criminal Justice; is a certified Mindful Eating coach; and a Registered Yoga Teacher. She has completed training in Child Abuse and Trauma; Social Media-based Interventions; End Of Life Doula training; and Using Deliberate Practice to Improve Effectiveness. 

Throughout her more than 15 years of practice, Barbara has sought to blend compassion with pragmatism; understanding that the root cause of most adjustment issues (i.e substance abuse and eating disorders) is the need, to both understand and forgive. Barbara’s particular expertise is connecting clients’ past to their present; guiding them through their history of trauma through group and individual therapy. Throughout her career, Barbara has addressed the needs of women; minority women, substance abusers; LGBT particularly gay men; and the formerly incarcerated; working with both Parole and Probation clients to prevent reoffending. Barbara has also been an Adjunct Professor at the City University of New York; Brooklyn College; and St. Francis College. 

In her free time, Barbara has begun playing guitar; is exploring song writing; dabbles in photography; and is a yoga practitioner. 

## SARAH KITT, LMSW 

Licensed Master Social Worker 

Sarah Kitt is a licensed therapist with a Master's in Social Work from the University of Southern California and a Master's in Applied Psychology from Yeshiva University. She also completed additional postgraduate training at the Ackerman Institute for the Family and is certified in Internal Family Systems (IFS). 
​ 
Her diverse professional background includes individual, couples, and family therapy (for clients dealing with anxiety, depression, eating disorders, attachment/interpersonal struggles, and family discourse) across a variety of settings (hospital, school, and foster care systems). 
​ 
Sarah’s practice is based on her belief in the value of authentic human connections and the power of the therapeutic relationship.  She’s especially interested in reshaping attachment patterns by working through past experiences, and supporting clients to use more effective coping skills in all stages of life. She incorporates a variety of evidence-based interventions according to each client’s needs which include Internal Family Systems (IFS), Narrative Therapy, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), Mentalization (MBT), and Emotion-focused couple’s therapy (EFT). 
​ 
In her free time, Sarah is an avid reader of mystery novels, enjoys gardening, and spending time with her family- especially her Maltipoo, Charlie Pepper.
