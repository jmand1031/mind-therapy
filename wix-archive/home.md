## The Mind Therapy Story 

Mind Therapy founder, Ally Nektalov, experienced difficulty finding an in-network therapist that she connected with. Ally painstakingly searched through therapists bios, scheduled appointments but was unsuccessful finding quality therapists that were accepting new clients and her insurance. Frustrated with this experience, Ally decided to start a group practice with experienced providers that accept a wide array of insurances using hassle- free, transparent scheduling. Mind Therapy was founded to make mental health service with quality providers accessible and simple. 
​ 
For convenience and accessibility, all of our services are provided via tele-health using simple, secure and HIPPA compliant software. Receiving quality therapy during your lunch break, while on vacation or at the comfort of your own home is now a reality. 
​ 
Mind Therapy is dedicated to help people overcome obstacles and empower them to live happier, healthier, and more fulfilling lives. We specialize in substance use and general mental health for adults and teens and focus on compassionate care while teaching skills and techniques to manage challenges.
