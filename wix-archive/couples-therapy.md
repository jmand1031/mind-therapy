## Couples Therapy That’s Covered by Insurance 

At Mind Therapy, we offer couples therapy that is covered by insurance, making it accessible and affordable for you and your partner. We are in-network with several major insurance providers, including United Healthcare, Oxford, Cigna, Oscar, Aetna, and Empire Blue Cross Blue Shield. This means that you can receive high-quality therapy while potentially reducing out-of-pocket costs. Our goal is to make it easier for you to prioritize your relationship and get the support you need without financial stress. If you have questions about coverage or need help understanding your benefits, our team is here to assist you. 

## How Couples Therapy Can Help Your Relationship 

Couples therapy at Mind Therapy can be a transformative experience for your relationship, offering a structured space to address and resolve conflicts, improve communication, and strengthen your emotional connection. Through guided sessions, you and your partner can explore underlying issues, understand each other's perspectives, and develop practical strategies for navigating challenges together. Our therapists provide a neutral and supportive environment, helping you both express your needs and concerns openly. Whether you’re dealing with ongoing disagreements, life transitions, or simply seeking to deepen your bond, couples therapy can facilitate growth, enhance mutual understanding, and foster a healthier, more fulfilling relationship.

## Our Approach to Couples Counseling 

Our approach to couples counseling uses exercises and lessons from Attachment Theory to bring attention and awareness to how our childhoods influence how we give and receive love. We are warm, yet directive and will provide concrete activities for the couple to build teamwork and cooperation in the relationship. We promote open and honest communication during therapy and are available to clients between sessions as needed. Our team of experts are here for you and ready to listen. Let’s begin the healing today and improve your relationship and intimacy with your partner.
