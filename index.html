Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Does Mind Therapy accept insurance?

Yes — Mind Therapy is in-network with major plans including Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Oxford, Empire BCBS, and Oscar. We verify your exact coverage before your first session, so you know what it will cost before you commit.

How much does therapy cost with insurance?

Most in-network clients pay only their plan's copay, typically $0–$50 per session. We confirm your exact cost before your first session, and self-pay rates are available if we're not in-network with your plan.

Is online therapy as effective as in-person therapy?

Yes — research consistently shows telehealth therapy is as effective as in-person care for anxiety, depression, and PTSD. Many clients find it easier to open up from a familiar space, and consistency improves without commutes.

Who can use Mind Therapy?

Anyone physically located in New York State. Our therapists are licensed in New York and see adults, couples, and teens — from New York City to Buffalo.

Can parents book therapy for their teen?

Yes — parents can book the first appointment on their teen's behalf. We'll explain how consent and confidentiality work for minors in New York before the first session.

How soon can I start?

Most clients book an appointment within days and begin sessions the same week. Evening and weekend appointments are available.

Our team

Meet all 13 of our licensed New York therapists

Every Mind Therapy clinician is licensed in New York and chosen for both expertise and warmth. Get matched based on your needs, your schedule, and your insurance.

Ally Nektalov

LCSW, Master CASAC · Director

Ally specializes in substance use and trauma using interpersonal psychoanalytic models. She earned her MSW from NYU, trained in psychoanalytic psychotherapy at the Alanson J. White Institute, and previously directed an outpatient substance-use clinic while teaching as an adjunct professor at NYU and Touro College. As Director, she oversees the practice and supervises the clinical team.

Kameeka Burke

MHC-P

Kameeka brings 7+ years supporting children, adolescents, families, and NYC school communities. She helps with emotional regulation, anxiety, self-esteem, behavioral concerns, and school-related stress, drawing on CBT, mindfulness, ACT, motivational interviewing, and trauma-informed care. She works best with ages 4–18, parents, and families navigating transitions.

Elizabeth Han

MHC-P

Elizabeth works across the lifespan — children, adolescents, adults, and older adults — on anxiety, life transitions, emotional regulation, and interpersonal challenges. She uses CBT, Gestalt therapy, and solution-focused therapy (and is pursuing EMDR training), with an active, collaborative style that balances reflection with meaningful engagement.

Bryonna Perperian

MHC-P

Bryonna supports clients through anxiety, depression, trauma, life transitions, relationship concerns, self-esteem, grief and loss, and addiction. Using CBT, person-centered therapy, and mindfulness practices, she creates a nonjudgmental environment that emphasizes client empowerment and collaborative healing.

Abigail Harrison

MHC-P

A former middle-school teacher with a master’s from Wake Forest University, Abigail brings deep empathy to mood and anxiety disorders, substance use, couples work, eating disorders, trauma, and identity and men’s issues. Her toolkit includes DBT, CBT, the Gottman Method, and motivational interviewing.

Tamlyn Freedman

LMSW

Tamlyn holds an MSW from NYU and a prior master’s in pharmaceutical sciences with a clinical research background. She takes a gentle, warm, strengths-based approach to anxiety, depression, life transitions, relationships, career changes, parenting, self-esteem, and grief — using CBT, ACT, solution-focused, and emotion-focused therapy.

Barbara Hoffmann

LCSW-R, PhD, CASAC

Licensed in New York and Florida with a PhD in criminal justice, Barbara brings 15+ years of experience serving women, minorities, LGBTQ+ clients, and formerly incarcerated individuals, alongside teaching at CUNY. She specializes in substance use, eating disorders, trauma, and adjustment issues — blending compassion with pragmatism by connecting past trauma to the present.

Sophia Alan

LCSW

Sophia (MSW, NYU) works with teens and young adults struggling with anxiety, depression, trauma, self-esteem, coping skills, and relationships, as well as children exposed to domestic violence. She combines CBT, mindfulness, and creative art and play therapy with parental coaching, prioritizing the therapeutic relationship as the foundation of healing.

Sarah Kitt

LMSW · IFS-certified

Sarah is certified in Internal Family Systems, with an MSW from USC, a master’s in applied psychology from Yeshiva University, and postgraduate training at the Ackerman Institute for the Family. Across hospital, school, and foster-care settings she has helped individuals, couples, and families with anxiety, depression, eating disorders, attachment struggles, and family discord — using IFS, narrative therapy, CBT, DBT, MBT, and EFT.

Bianca E. Ortiz

LMHC

Bianca is a compassionate therapist focused on trauma-related anxiety and depression, motherhood transitions, postpartum adjustment, and women’s life transitions. Through CBT, IFS, and solution-focused therapy, she helps women restore emotional balance and rediscover confidence with a nurturing, attuned presence.

Maria Cicchilli

LMHC, PhD

With 20+ years of individual and couples therapy — and a license as a psychologist in Argentina — Maria works with teenagers and adults on anxiety, depression, family conflict, self-esteem, and personality disorders. Her psychodynamic, culture-aware approach integrates mind-body awareness, offered fluently in English, Spanish, or Italian.

Alessandra Scalia

LMHC, NCC, CASAC

Alessandra helps clients facing anxiety, depression, low self-esteem, addiction and substance use, trauma, and life transitions. Her CBT-based work focuses on thought patterns, stress management, mindfulness, and self-care — empowering clients through education and healthy coping skills.

Constance Grey

LCSW

With 40+ years working with adults and couples, accredited postgraduate psychoanalytic training, and active membership in the Society for Modern Psychoanalysis, Constance is an insightful listener who supports clients through anxiety, panic attacks, depression, trauma, and relationship issues. Her person-centered foundation blends CBT with insight-oriented work — she believes it is never too late to change and grow.
